    02 · How a round works

    From one trade to five coins in a wallet.

    Pod collects the fee. A keeper wallet turns it into the basket and delivers it, on a fixed 15 minute clock.

    1. 01
      Fees arriveEach trade pays Pod’s 2% fee. 70% of it lands in the keeper wallet as SOL.
    2. 02
      The five are pickedPod coins by market cap, keeping only the ones that pass every safety filter.
    3. 03
      Equal slices are boughtThe pot is split evenly and each slice buys one coin through Jupiter.
    4. 04
      Shares are worked outWhatever actually arrived is split by POD5 balance among wallets holding $30 or more.
    5. 05
      Coins are sentStraight to your wallet. Tiny first-time shares wait until they cover the account rent.

    What have I received?

    Paste any Solana address to see what has been delivered, what is waiting, and whether the wallet clears the hold line. Delivered and pending amounts are looked up locally in the public ledger. Only the qualification check asks an RPC node for the wallet’s POD5 balance.
