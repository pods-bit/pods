Project plan · naviklabs

Hold one coin. Receive the top five on Pod.

An ember5-style coin launched on orcapod.fun. Every 15 minutes, its creator fee buys the five biggest Pod coins in equal slices and sends them to POD5 holders in proportion to their holdings. Nothing to claim or stake.

01 · Context

ember5 proves the model. Pod has the plumbing.

Reference · ember5.xyz

How EMBER5 works

  • Trading fees go to a keeper wallet.
  • Each round buys the top five embercurve coins by market cap, after filtering, in equal slices through Jupiter.
  • Wallets holding at least $30 of EMBER5 are credited by balance, using the amounts that actually arrived.
  • Coins are pushed to holders. A small share waits until it's worth more than the token-account rent.
  • Pool vaults and other program-owned addresses are excluded.
Our base · orcapod.fun

What Pod gives us

  • Each coin gets its own Orca Whirlpool from the first block, with liquidity locked.
  • A flat 2% fee on every trade: 70% to the creator's module and 30% to the platform, which buys and burns $POD.
  • The Harbor & Crew module pays that 70% to a wallet about every 15 minutes, in SOL.
  • An open JSON API with market cap, liquidity, holders, volume and a “suspect” flag for every coin.

Pod's own “Cargo” basket module can only buy whitelisted tokenized stocks plus JUP. It can't hold Pod memes, so we run our own keeper.

02 · Fee flow

From one trade to five coins in a wallet.

Pod handles fee collection. Our keeper turns that SOL into the basket and delivers it.

On $1,000 of POD5 trading, the keeper receives $14 in SOL. Three costs come out before holders receive anything: each target coin's own 2% Pod fee on our buy, price impact, and network fees plus account rent.

03 · Basket rules

Up to five coins, ranked by market cap.

If fewer than five pass every filter, the pot is split between the ones that do.

  • Not flagged suspect or paused by Pod, and at least 24 hours old.
  • Pool liquidity of at least $5K. Each buy is capped at about 1% of the pool, and anything over the cap rolls into the next round.
  • At least 50 holders, $1K of 24-hour volume and 20 trades in 24 hours.
  • No freeze authority, no mint authority, and no risky Token-2022 extensions.
  • A Jupiter route exists for SOL to the coin, with price impact of 3% or less on top of Pod's 2% fee.
  • Coins are identified by mint address, never by ticker. Pod already has three ORCATs and two PODCATs.

What this means today

With Pod's current liquidity, only one or two coins would pass. We should market POD5 as “up to five”. The basket fills out as Pod grows.

04 · One round

Every 15 minutes, six steps.

A fixed cycle. We dropped ember5's 75% reach gate and kept only the guards that stop fees being wasted.

01

Budget. Pot = keeper SOL − 0.05 SOL operating reserve − estimated network fees. If the pot is under 0.05 SOL, skip the round and carry it over.

02

Snapshot. Read POD5 holders and weight each by balance. Exclude pool vaults and other program addresses, the keeper, the deployer and Pod's wallets. Require a minimum holding of $30 (tunable).

03

Buy. One Jupiter swap per slice. Credit the actual balance change. A failed or over-impact buy rolls its slice to the next round.

04

Credit. Each wallet gets floor(received × balance ÷ total) in raw units. Leftover dust carries into the next round. The round is written to the ledger in one database transaction.

05

Deliver. If a wallet already has an account for that coin, the share is sent immediately. Otherwise it builds up until it's worth more than the ~0.002 SOL account rent, then the account is opened with the rent taken from the share.

06

Publish. Write a round receipt (buys, signatures, recipients) and the pending-share ledger as public JSON.

Guard 01

Liquidity floor

Buying into a $12 pool mostly moves the price. Coins below the floor are skipped.

Guard 02

Hold back small shares

Opening a token account costs about $0.24. A first send of five coins to 200 holders would cost about $240 in rent, against a pot of about $15 a round at $100K daily volume.

Guard 03

Exclude pool wallets

POD5's own pool holds a large share of the supply. Paying by raw balance would pay the pool, not people.

Crash safety: each step saves its state before sending anything. After a restart, the keeper checks pending signatures on-chain, so nothing is bought or sent twice.

06 · Launch on Pod

Four steps to go live.

01

Two wallets. A deployer that signs the Pod launch, and a keeper hot wallet that holds only the working float. The keeper key lives in a secret manager, never in the repo.

02

Launch at orcapod.fun/create. Pair with SOL, choose the Harbor & Crew module, and set the payout wallet to the keeper. The module can't be changed after launch. Cost is about 0.05 SOL in rent.

03

Configure. Add the POD5 mint to the keeper config. Add POD5's pool, Pod's platform and treasury wallets, and the deployer to the exclusion list.

04

Optional. Ask @orcapodfun for the “curated” badge. POD5 sends volume to their coins and burns $POD through their 30% cut, so our incentives line up.

09 · Risks

What could go wrong.

RiskMitigation
Thin ecosystemOnly one or two coins pass today. Market it as “up to five”. The liquidity floor stops us moving prices against ourselves.
Basket gamingSomeone pumps a micro-cap into the top five to sell into our buys. The age, holder, volume, liquidity and suspect filters plus the impact cap limit this.
Pod dependencyIf Harbor payouts stall or the API changes, nothing flows in. Alert when no inflow arrives for several hours, and fall back to chain and Jupiter data.
Hot walletKeep only the float, alert on unexpected outflows, and add a kill switch.
LegalA fee-funded asset basket paid to holders should get a quick legal review before marketing.
10 · Verification

How we'll know it works.

01

Unit tests (cd keeper && npx vitest): credits sum to received minus carry, the minimum-pot skip, rent gating, dust carry, filter edge cases, and resume after a crash at every step.

02

Dry run (npm run round -- --dry-run): print the basket, quotes, eligible wallets and planned transfers from live Pod and Jupiter data, without sending.

03

Local fork. Clone the target mints, pools and Jupiter into solana-test-validator (or Surfpool), run a full round and check recipient balances.

04

Mainnet canary. Cap the pot at about 0.2 SOL, check buy and transfer signatures on Solscan, and confirm the published receipt matches the chain.

05

Website. Compare live numbers with Pod's market data and test the wallet checker against a known holder.
